    About the club

    We design, build, and program competition robots, and we share that knowledge with younger students through mentorship and outreach.

    Programs

    Currently, we are organized into two divisions and an outreach program.

    • Division C

      The high school competition team. PiE in 2026–2027, VEX beginning in 2027–2028. Active

    • Division B

      The middle school program, focused on hands-on instruction, with optional competition. Planned for 2027–2028

    • Open Bench

      Guidance in robotics and STEM for any student, regardless of membership. In development

    Competitions

    During the 2026–2027 season, Division C will compete in the Pioneers in Engineering (PiE) Robotics Competition. PiE is organized by students at the University of California, Berkeley, and pairs each participating high school team with university mentors.

    Beginning in 2027–2028, we intend to compete in the VEX V5 Robotics Competition at the regional level. Our fundraising and grant applications for this transition are under way.

    Mentorship

    Members of Division C also serve as instructors and technical mentors. They will lead workshops in computer-aided design (CAD), electronics, and programming, and will mentor students in Division B once that program begins.

    Planned for 2027–2028

    Division B

    Division B will be our middle school program, beginning in the 2027–2028 school year.

    Program

    Division B will emphasize instruction and hands-on engineering practice. Students will build foundational skills in mechanical design, electronics, and programming under the guidance of high school mentors.

    Competition will be optional. Students who wish to compete may enter entry-level VEX tournaments.

    Preparation

    • Host demonstrations and workshops at the middle school
    • Develop the curriculum and secure a faculty advisor
    • Arrange a regular meeting space and mentor schedule
    • Secure funding for equipment and competition fees

    Future expansion

    We are also considering STEM workshops for elementary school students, including introductory LEGO and VEX IQ modules.

    Inquiries

    Families interested in Division B may contact us at albanyroboticsleadership@gmail.com.

    Pioneers in Engineering

    Pioneers in Engineering (PiE) is a student organization at the University of California, Berkeley, founded in 2008. Each year it runs a robotics competition for high schools across the Bay Area.

    Date
    Early 2027. The exact date will be announced.
    Format
    Teams receive a robotics kit and are paired with UC Berkeley student mentors. Over the season, each team designs, builds, and programs a robot, then competes in a final tournament.
    Scale
    More than 700 high school students from over 30 Bay Area schools have competed in PiE.

    VEX V5 Robotics Competition

    The VEX V5 Robotics Competition is run by the Robotics Education & Competition Foundation. With more than 20,000 registered teams in over 50 countries, VEX is one of the largest robotics programs in the world. A new game is released each spring and played through the following school year.

    Our first season
    2027–2028
    Format
    Matches are played on a 12-by-12-foot field by two alliances of two teams each. Each match opens with a 15-second autonomous period, followed by 1 minute and 45 seconds of driver control.
    Season
    Local qualifying tournaments in the fall and winter, followed by regional championships and the VEX Robotics World Championship in the spring.
    Current game
    Override (2026–2027). The 2027–2028 game will be announced in spring 2027.

    Open Bench

    Open Bench is our outreach program. We offer guidance in robotics and the wider fields of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics to any student, whether or not they are a member.

    Questions

    Students may send questions about projects, coursework, programming, computer-aided design, electronics, or competitive robotics to albanyroboticsleadership@gmail.com. A member of our team will respond.

    When a question falls outside the team's expertise, we will help identify an appropriate resource.

    Workshops and demonstrations

    We plan to offer hands-on demonstrations and workshops, beginning at the middle school. Dates will be published on the Meeting and Competition Dates page.

    Scope

    Robotics draws on mathematics, physics, programming, and design. Questions in any of these areas, and in science and engineering more broadly, are welcome.
