Running now

Div C is our high school team.

This is the part of the club that builds a robot and competes with it. This year that means PiE. Next year we plan to move to VEX.

This year: PiE

PiE stands for Pioneers in Engineering. It's a student organization at UC Berkeley that runs a robotics competition for Bay Area high schools each spring. Teams get a kit and work with Berkeley student mentors, then build a robot and compete against other schools.

It's a good place to start. It's close to home, and it lets us learn how to design, build, and program a robot before we take on a bigger competition.

Our goals for this first season are to build a robot we're proud of, compete, and do well.

Next year: VEX

In 2027–28 we plan to enter the VEX V5 Robotics Competition and compete at regional tournaments.

VEX costs a lot more than PiE. A team needs its own kit and parts, pays a registration fee, and pays an entry fee for each event. So part of this year goes to raising money and applying for grants early, before that season starts.

Teaching

High school members also teach. We plan to run workshops for younger students on CAD, electronics, and programming, and to mentor the middle school program once it opens.

Planned for 2027–28

Div B will be our middle school program.

It isn't running yet. We plan to open it in the 2027–28 school year, once the high school team has a season behind it.

How it will work

Div B is for learning first. Meetings will be hands-on: building, wiring, and programming, with high school members teaching and mentoring.

Competing will be optional. Students who want to can enter beginner-level VEX tournaments. Students who would rather just build can do that.

What has to happen first

  • Run demos and workshops at the middle school to see who's interested
  • Plan what we'll teach, and find a teacher advisor
  • Get a regular meeting space and a mentor schedule
  • Line up funding

Later on

If Div B goes well, we'd like to try workshops for elementary students too, starting with LEGO and VEX IQ.

Interested?

If you're a middle school student or parent and want to hear when Div B opens, email us at albanyroboticsleadership@gmail.com.

Getting started

Open Bench

Open Bench is how we share what we know. It's for any student who's curious about robotics or any other part of STEM, whether or not they're in the club.

Ask us something

Stuck on a project? Wondering how to get started with coding, CAD, or electronics? Curious what a robotics competition is like? Email us and someone on the team will answer.

If we don't know, we'll say so and help you find someone who does.
