Robotics at Albany High School.

Albany Robotics is a student-led engineering organization at Albany High School in Albany, California. The club will compete in the Pioneers in Engineering Robotics Competition this season, with plans to enter VEX competition and open a middle school program the following year.

About the club

Albany Robotics designs, builds, and programs competition robots, and shares that knowledge with younger students through mentorship and outreach.

Programs

The club is organized into two divisions and an outreach program.

  • Division C

    The high school competition team. PiE in 2026–2027, VEX beginning in 2027–2028. Active

  • Division B

    The middle school program, focused on hands-on instruction, with optional competition. Planned for 2027–2028

  • Open Bench

    Guidance in robotics and STEM for any student, regardless of membership. In development

Competitions

During the 2026–2027 season, Division C will compete in the Pioneers in Engineering (PiE) Robotics Competition. PiE is organized by students at the University of California, Berkeley, and pairs each participating high school team with university mentors.

Beginning in 2027–2028, the team intends to compete in the VEX V5 Robotics Competition at the regional level. Fundraising and grant applications for this transition are under way.

Mentorship

Members of Division C also serve as instructors and technical mentors. They will lead workshops in computer-aided design (CAD), electronics, and programming, and will mentor students in Division B once that program begins.

Planned for 2027–2028

Division B

Division B will be the middle school program of Albany Robotics, beginning in the 2027–2028 school year.

Program

Division B will emphasize instruction and hands-on engineering practice. Students will build foundational skills in mechanical design, electronics, and programming under the guidance of high school mentors.

Competition will be optional. Students who wish to compete may enter entry-level VEX tournaments.

Preparation

  • Host demonstrations and workshops at the middle school
  • Develop the curriculum and secure a faculty advisor
  • Arrange a regular meeting space and mentor schedule
  • Secure funding for equipment and competition fees

Future expansion

Albany Robotics is also considering STEM workshops for elementary school students, including introductory LEGO and VEX IQ modules.

Inquiries

Families interested in Division B may contact the leadership team at albanyroboticsleadership@gmail.com.

In development

Open Bench

Open Bench is the outreach program of Albany Robotics. It offers guidance in robotics and the wider fields of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics to any student, whether or not they are a member of the club.

Questions

Students may send questions about projects, coursework, programming, computer-aided design, electronics, or competitive robotics to albanyroboticsleadership@gmail.com. A member of the team will respond.

When a question falls outside the team's expertise, we will help identify an appropriate resource.

Workshops and demonstrations

Albany Robotics plans to offer hands-on demonstrations and workshops, beginning at the middle school. Dates will be published on the Meeting and Competition Dates page.

Scope

Robotics draws on mathematics, physics, programming, and design. Questions in any of these areas, and in science and engineering more broadly, are welcome.
